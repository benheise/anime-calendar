How to read this

Dates and times are North American streaming release times in US Eastern — when the subtitled episode actually goes live on the platform, which typically runs 30–90 minutes after the Japanese TV broadcast. They were verified against AnimeSchedule.net's tracked releases during the premiere week (Jul 6–12, 2026); odd-looking minutes like “9:01” or “7:31” are that tracker's convention for “immediately after broadcast.” Shows with no NA stream tracked yet show the Japanese broadcast time converted to Eastern instead. “TBA” means a precise time (or, for a few shows, a platform) hasn’t been published for North America; titles tagged TBA as a platform are announced Fall 2026 titles whose North American service hasn’t been confirmed yet. Currently airing shows with no stream on the covered platforms are not listed. Schedules shift often (delays, double-episodes); confirm on the platform before a premiere. Coverage is the Japanese seasonal slate plus Crunchyroll's announced international pickups as they're confirmed for North America — donghua (Crowned in a Hundred Days, twice weekly; Full-Time Magister S7, Tuesday nights; the Lord of Mysteries specials, June; Link Click Season 3, August) and French animation (Dreamland, October).

The calendar tracks the release schedule, not the catalog: a completed season stays in the Cards view marked “complete” — and remains fully watchable on its platform — but leaves the weekly grid after its finale. The weekly schedule models each show's run — a title appears from its premiere through its finale, then drops off the calendar. Finale dates come from episode counts: where a studio has announced one, the confirmed count is used (shown as e.g. “12 eps”); otherwise a standard single-cour estimate of ~12 episodes is assumed and marked “est.”, since most Summer 2026 titles haven't published a length this early. The calendar runs from the season's first premiere through the end of 2026; most shows finish inside that window (two-cour titles like The Exiled Heavy Knight, at 26 episodes, run to late December), while a few that premiere in late fall continue into early 2027 beyond the year-end edge.

Fall 2026: the season is included as premiere dates get confirmed. As of early July only a handful of Fall shows have locked dates (those appear on the grid); many more are announced but unscheduled and are listed beneath the calendar as “premiere date not yet scheduled” — they'll slot into place as studios set dates. Winter time is handled automatically: US daylight saving ends November 1, and because these releases are anchored to Japan's clock (which has no DST), every slot lands one hour earlier on the US clock from that date. The weekly schedule shows the adjusted EST time for any date on or after Nov 1 and labels those weeks; browse cards show each show's premiere-date (EDT) time.
